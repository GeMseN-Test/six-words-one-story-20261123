One complete story in exactly six words — a wedding, a mistaken identity, and a choice.

> **Married the wrong twin. Stayed anyway.**

## The story

Six words, one whole life implied: the ceremony happened, the wrong
person stood at the altar, and the narrator chose to stay anyway.
A twist in the middle, a decision at the end, and everything
before and after left for the reader to fill in.

## How it works

`main.py` (Python standard library only) writes the story to
`/output/entry.txt` — deterministic, no trailing newline, 6 words,
38 characters, inside the 1–60 character gate.

```sh
python3 main.py
cat /output/entry.txt
```
